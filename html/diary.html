Topics in this entry: Same website syndrome, lost and found, music rec.

2 hours spent making this blog page, two hours of sleep lost... Slept at 3 AM, woke up 11:30 AM. This is a personal warning to never lose sleep over the website again.

There's a big problem that my website is suffering from: "Same Website Syndrome". Horizontal nav bar at the top, some form of marquee right after, and then the vertically scrolling contents and stuff. 1px black or blue borders on everything. It's clean and minimalistic. Not only that, it has a stark navy blue "aesthetic" palette and a background that's abstract and wavy and blue as well. Not only is it basically my site, I've seen similar looking ones too. It basically describes Joosh's website, a website I do really admire.

Of course, there isn't really anything wrong with it, I am proud of my website. But it feels like I haven't shown who I really am as a person other than the love of the color blue. When I first discovered the indie web, I thought I could find myself or figure out who I am as a person. But it really feels like I haven't so far. This is quite harsh, because I know it's only v2. It won't even be my final form layout. Cheers to the new year though, the big 2025 is in the past.

This past month, I've lost some very important things only to find them again. First was my favorite pen, the Uni Jetstream. I was convinced that I dropped it at the library after a study session, and was extremely devastated. Only to find it in my tote bag 2 weeks later while trying to look for my pencil case after I lost that too.

I lost my nicest water bottle only to find it in the robotics workshop on a table, a bit more scratched up. I swear I'm not that absentminded. It has been a bit of a rough month though, so...

I really like music. It's been really cold lately so I've been working from my bed. I'm worried about the reprecussions of slumping over like a shrimp all the time though. It can't be good for my internal organs at all.

I recently got a last.fm account, but the only widgets I see being implemented on the indie web are the "last played" widgets. I want to figure out how to show my favorite songs somehow.

If you read all of that yap, thanks! I hope you enjoy the music I've been enjoying this month below...
