I saw the Chicago Symphony Orchestra today. They played two pieces; I am very distraught to say that I fell asleep during the second half of the first piece, "apotheosis". That's a nice word. The second piece was Symphonie Fantastique Op. 14 by Berlioz. It was thoroughly enjoyable to watch and listen to. The second movement was definitely my cup of tea. I felt the winds in the 3rd movement, and enjoyed the ranz des vaches the oboes played. The 5th movement Dream of a Night of the Sabbath was pleasantly ominous, and the various techniquesnused to depict the strange noises, laughter, and cries were very on point. By the last few chords, I was practically begging in my mind for the piece to not end.

great stuff. I nearly froze to death walking towards the auditorium though. I had just showered after crew practice (painful aerobic threshold) and naively thought the weather would be forgiving, so I decided to wear thin wide leg sweatpants and a nice blue sweater. The walk back wasn't as bad because I was so warm from all the excitement. Thinking back, I missed the encore piece they played. Damn! I wish I saw it.

The french horn is a difficult instrument. Practice not forgiving like a piano or violin. Days without practice deteriorates the mouth "embouchure", 1 step forward 4 steps back. The sounds made by a well played french horn are majestic. Buttery-smooth and impactful, it brings tears to my eyes to be able to witness such beauty.

I chose the french horn because I wanted to be niche- but not too niche. In 5th grade, I tried the euphonium (baritone). "What the hell is that?" People asked; it was too niche for me. Because of how niche it was, I was under the misconception that I could not play euphonium in a symphony orchestra (my goal for high school). I bid the mini-tuba goodbye and set my sights on the french horn.

In middle school, I thought I was all that. I made All-State for middle school. All-State is basically a band/orchestra/ensemble public school students in a state can audition for. The best students get the spots for each ensemble. I shed my blood, sweat, and tears for that lowkey. But I'm not some kind of horn genius, and I didn't practice enough over the summer leading to high school. Not only that, but I never had innately the strength to play in the "high" range of the horn.

The high range of the horn is extremely difficult for me to play. I really hate it because I'm supposed to be able to play it in the first place, but I can't and it sounds horrid. I can't reach the notes with ease yet. Where I stand right now, my confidence in my playing is basically cooked. I'm scared to even play mid-range notes during rehearsal. It's honestly embarrassing, considering everything.

I have been trying (not hard enough) to fix this issue, but I haven't really had the time to practice either. One could argue that if I never wasted a single hour of my day, I could have easily practiced an hour or two. I'm too tired for ts bro. Even here with my cooked self-esteem and playing, I know I'm not going to quit. I aspire to have the beautiful horn sounds that all my peers do. I don't think I'll stop until then.

If you read through all that yap, have some music. The potential carcinogens in diet coke are scaring me. I think I'll drink more san pellegrino and kombucha. I really want a nice cold drink even though it's so cold out.

I would have never started developing a website if it weren't for two things:

  1. A youtube video documenting the indie web
  2. A photo of the Persona 3 Reload menu UI

I fell in love with the menu.

It was in 8th grade, probably, when I first witnessed it. I was in a lowkey depressed, angsty headspace (not really). It was also my first concrete interaction with the Persona franchise. That yap session is for another day. The design was so cool and tranquil with the floating water and main character staring into space while listening to music while the typography was stylishly messy and chaotic. A vast sea of blue with shocks of red. This UI really spoke to me. Then, a spark of genius came to me- Why didn't I just make this UI into a personal website? That would be really cool.

I started on Neocities, coding directly into the given code editor. Yikes. As expected, the website was nothing more than an index page and looked really crappy because there was basically nothing on it. So I took a break. After half a year, I decided to consume websites other people had made to inherit scraps of their web-making genius. Those websites can mostly be found on my links page above. During my manifest destiny winter arc, I had probably pressed Inspect Elements and View Page Source at least two thousand times.

This website represents the meager fruits of my labor. It's not perfect, but I have at least put something down that I am somewhat satisfied with. An imperfect representation of myself. I can live with it for now. Then one night scrolling through Pinterest in desperate search of inspiration, I found a beautiful image. I fell in love again.

"It's beautiful. This UI is beautiful. I need this in my life. This moves me, brings me to tears. Wow." All thoughts running through my head in that moment. This is the kind of effect that I want to create for myself. This is what I will create- one day. Dinner is not served yet.
