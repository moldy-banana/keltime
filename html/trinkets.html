>> my every day carry

Usually I carry two bags. The North Face Recon 30L Backpack in Canyon Dark Dust Heather and the Speedo Teamster 1.0. They both have plentiful space & lots of pockets. My Recon bag carries all of my stationery, including my favorites:

>>> stationery

my monographs... i really like how sleek and fancy they look but the tip of the pencil really isn't doing it for me. as the pencil that got me into stationery, i'm disappointed in how easily the tip bends. the top eraser sometimes falls off too. the pencil is a little bit heavy bc of metal weight inside that can extend the lead when the pencil is shaken. this makes it easy to do pencil gymnastics between your fingers.

the uni kuru toga is really easy to use but the body feels pretty cheap. it's a light pencil. i wish i could buy a kuru toga dive or a heavier one but those are crazy expensive. for the amount of times i've dropped this pencil, i don't think the tip has bent too much, unlike the monograph (probably bc kuru toga is lighter). i only wish the eraser was bigger.

uni jetstream 0.5mm. it's cheap-ish and feels pretty okay for being cheap. i've gone through 3 or 4 of the refill inks already and the ink is good but i still see skips sometimes. i would like to try a 0.7mm version of this.
