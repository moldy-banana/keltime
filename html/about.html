// MY CHUNGUS LIFE

Hi, my name is kel. This is my website that I plan to forever personalize and add to. The "test" tab will contain the expansion plans. "Coding" a website is pretty fun, but now it feels like I'm stuck without anything to show. I like sketching, eating food, learning new things, music, cool stuff, and good graphic design. Updates to this website will be quite far apart because of school, studying, and sports.

I wanted to learn how to code cool stuff (maybe with javascript or python?), but got distracted by making a website. I used to play the piano, but two hands are complicated so I play french horn now, which may or may not have caused me 10x the amount of stress. I like rhythm games! My favorite is Taiko no Tatsujin, I hope to buy one of those big fancy drums one day.
